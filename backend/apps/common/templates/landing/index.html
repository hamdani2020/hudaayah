Build a daily habit with the Quran.

Hudaayah helps you understand and remember the Quran through short, gentle lessons. Read each ayah with its translation, learn it word by word, listen to the recitation, and review what you learned when it is due.

Made for steady, daily learning

Whether you are learning the meaning or working on memorization, each lesson takes a few minutes and builds on the last.

📖

Short daily lessons

Each lesson covers a few ayat, so it fits into a busy day.

🔤

Word by word

See each word with its meaning and transliteration, alongside the full translation.

🎧

Listen and repeat

Play the recitation for each ayah and loop it as many times as you need.

🔁

Gentle reviews

Words you have learned come back for review just before you are likely to forget them.

🔥

Your own pace

A streak and XP help you keep the habit. They are personal, with no leaderboards or comparisons.

🌿

Encouraging, never harsh

Mistakes are part of learning. Feedback is kind and points you to try again.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in with Google

    Your progress is saved to your account, so you can pick up where you left off.

  2. 2

    Read and listen

    Start a lesson with the ayah in Arabic, its translation, and the recitation.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Answer short exercises: choose the meaning, put the words in order, and recall what you heard.

  4. 4

    Review

    Come back each day to review words that are due and continue your journey.

Hudaayah starts with Surah Al-Fatihah and the opening ayat of Surah Al-Baqarah. More surahs are planned.

A look inside

Respect for the Quran comes first

The Arabic text, translations and recitations come from Quran Foundation and are shown exactly as provided. Translations are always labeled as translations. Our lessons and exercises are kept clearly separate from the Quran text.
