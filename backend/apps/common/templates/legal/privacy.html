Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

Hudaayah ("we", "the app") is a Quran learning app that helps you build a daily habit of studying the Quran through short lessons. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture URL. We use these to create and identify your account.
  • Learning activity. Your lesson attempts, answers to exercises, scores, completed lessons, review schedule, experience points (XP), streak, daily goal and daily activity.
  • Preferences. Settings you choose in the app.
  • Session data. Sign-in session tokens. We store only hashed copies on our servers. On your device they are kept in the platform's secure storage.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs such as request time, request ID and IP address, used for security and troubleshooting. We do not log sign-in tokens or secrets.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To grade exercises, track progress, XP and streaks, and schedule reviews.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the app.
  • To respond to your messages.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show advertising.

Third parties

  • Google Sign-In. Used to authenticate you. Google's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in.
  • Quran Foundation. Quran text, translations and audio come from the Quran Foundation Content API. Our server requests this content; we do not send your personal information with those requests. Audio files may be streamed to your device from Quran Foundation's servers, which can see your device's IP address.
  • Hosting. Our servers and database run on Amazon Web Services.

We share information with authorities only if the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or later). Access to our systems is limited and secrets are kept outside the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and learning data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data yourself at any time from the Profile screen in the app (Delete Account), or ask us to; see how to delete your account.

Children

Hudaayah is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
