Delete your Hudaayah account

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Hudaayah and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then confirm Delete.

Your account and data are permanently deleted immediately, and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

Can't open the app?

If you no longer have the app installed, email {{ contact_email }} from the email address of the Google account you used with Hudaayah, with the subject "Delete my Hudaayah account". We will delete your account within 30 days of confirming the request.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile (email, display name, profile picture URL).
  • Your lesson attempts, answers, progress, review schedule, XP, streak, daily activity and the record of days you opened the app.
  • Your sign-in sessions.

What may remain

Routine server backups and security logs (for example request time and IP address) may keep limited data for a short period until they expire. We do not use them to restore a deleted account. Deleting your account in Hudaayah does not delete your Google account.
